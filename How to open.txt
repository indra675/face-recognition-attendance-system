Open a terminal in C:\PROJECT\Webapp
Open File Explorer, go to C:\PROJECT\Webapp (type this in the address bar and press Enter). Click the address bar, type cmd, press Enter to open a terminal there.
2
Activate the environment
Type: venv\Scripts\activate and press Enter. You'll see (venv) appear at the start of the line.
3
Run the app
Type: python app.py and press Enter. Wait for 'Running on http://127.0.0.1:5000', then open Chrome/Edge and go to that address. Keep the black window open while you use the app — closing it stops the app.
